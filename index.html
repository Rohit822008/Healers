Patient Visitor Management System for Hospitals

Hospital entrances oversee a continuous flow of visitors seeking access to patients. This platform structures visitor registration, department hours, and exit tracking into an organized workflow for safer hospital operations.

Essential Visitor Guidelines

1. Digital Check-In: Every visitor must register valid identity details and select a patient before entry.

2. Entry Pass: Carry your generated Visitor Pass and QR ID for verification at ward checkpoints.

3. Visiting Hours: Follow assigned ward and ICU time slots to protect patient recovery.
