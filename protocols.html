Hospital Health & Safety Protocols

Mandatory hygiene, infection control, and conduct rules for all hospital visitors.

1. Infection Control & Hygiene

  • Sanitize hands before entering and after leaving any patient ward.
  • Wear a surgical mask inside ICU, surgical, and isolation units.
  • Visitors with fever, cough, or cold symptoms are not permitted inside wards.

2. Ward Conduct & Patient Privacy

  • Keep mobile phones on silent mode across all hospital corridors.
  • Photography or video recording inside treatment areas is strictly prohibited.
  • Outside food, flowers, and unapproved items are restricted near patient beds.

3. Biomedical Waste & Emergency Safety

  • Dispose of used masks and tissues only in designated hospital waste bins.
  • Keep fire exits, stretchers, and corridor pathways clear at all times.
  • Follow nurse and security instructions immediately during emergency drills.
